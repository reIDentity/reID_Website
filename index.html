FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Contact the team.

What is reID?

reID is your digital identity passport: one verified account to sign in to apps, hold cash and crypto, create Virtual IDs, share your profile and earn from your network.

What is a Virtual ID?

A separate identity you create inside reID, for shopping, travel or family. Each one can have its own email, phone number and debit card, so your real details stay private.

How does cashback work?

When you or anyone in your legacy network completes an eligible transaction with a reID merchant, you earn a share of the cashback. Claim it whenever you like, with no holding period.

How does verification work?

Scan your passport and complete a quick liveness check with our regulated KYC provider, or stake tokens to lift limits. You verify once and every connected app recognises it.

What is reCorporate?

The business version of reID: a desktop workspace for corporate identity, employees, roles and permissions, salaries, fiat and crypto accounts, and employee cashback.

How do I add Sign in with reID to my app?

Request API keys from your reCorporate account, install the reID SDK from npm or use any OpenID Connect library, and add the button.

Who can become an Ambassador?

Anyone with a verified reID who completes the Academy and passes the assessment. Apply from inside the reID app.
