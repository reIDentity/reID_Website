FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Contact the team.

What is reID?

reID is your digital identity passport: one verified account to sign in to apps, hold cash and crypto, share your profile and earn from your network.

Is my data private?

Yes. You choose what each app can see. Apps receive verified claims, like "identity verified", rather than your documents.

How does verification work?

Scan your passport and complete a quick liveness check with our regulated KYC provider, or stake tokens to lift limits. You verify once and every connected app recognises it.

How do I earn with the legacy network?

Share your reID QR. Everyone who joins through it becomes part of your legacy, and a share of the transactions your network makes, across up to 10 levels, is paid to your wallet.

What is reCorporate?

reCorporate is the business version of reID: a desktop workspace for corporate identity, employees, roles and permissions, salaries, fiat and crypto accounts, and employee cashback.

How do I add Sign in with reID to my app?

Request API keys from your reCorporate account, install the reID SDK from npm or use any OpenID Connect library, and add the button.

Who can become an Ambassador?

Anyone with a verified reID who completes the Academy and passes the assessment. Apply from inside the reID app.
