01

Who we are

reID ("reID", "we", "us") is a digital identity platform operated by Desert Tech Cloud Services LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use the reID apps, reCorporate, Sign in with reID and reidentity.net (together, the "Services").

02

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, username (for example name@reid), email, phone number and profile details you choose to add.
  • Verification data: identity document images, liveness checks and verification results, processed by our regulated KYC provider.
  • Wallet and transaction data: wallet addresses, balances, transfers, cashback and legacy network activity.
  • Corporate data: company details, employees, roles and permissions you add in reCorporate.
  • Device and usage data: device type, app version, IP address, log and security events.
  • Communications: messages you send to support and, if you use reID Chat, the messages you exchange.
03

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your reID and keep you signed in with passkeys, wallets or social accounts.
  • To verify your identity and meet anti-money-laundering and fraud-prevention duties.
  • To process payments, cashback, legacy rewards, Virtual IDs and virtual cards.
  • To let you share your verified profile and sign in to connected apps, only with your approval.
  • To improve the Services, provide support and send service messages.
04

Sharing with connected apps

When you use Sign in with reID, the connected app only receives the details you approve on the consent screen, such as your name, email or verified status. You can review and remove connected apps at any time in Settings.

05

Who we share data with

  • Regulated identity verification, payment, card-issuing and telecom partners that help deliver the Services.
  • Cloud hosting and security providers under contract and confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

06

International transfers

Your data is primarily processed in the UAE. Where it is transferred abroad, we use safeguards required by applicable law, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law.

07

How long we keep data

We keep personal data while your account is active and afterwards for as long as required to meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations. Verification records may be retained for the period required by anti-money-laundering rules.

08

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Contact us at privacy@reidentity.net to exercise these rights.

09

Security

We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, passkey and biometric sign-in, access controls and continuous monitoring. Zero-knowledge verification lets apps confirm facts about you without seeing the underlying documents.

10

Children

The Services are not intended for children below the age required by local law to open an account. Family features such as Virtual IDs for family members are managed by the adult account holder.

11

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will notify you of material changes in the app or by email. Questions: privacy@reidentity.net, Desert Tech Cloud Services LLC, Dubai, UAE.

This document is provided for information and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.
