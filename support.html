02

Account and sign-in

  • Lost your phone? Sign in on a new device with your passkey, wallet or social account, then remove the old device in Settings → Security.
  • Can't verify? Use good lighting, a valid passport and remove phone cases that cover the camera.
  • Want to leave? See delete your account.
03

Payments and wallet

  • Payments to name@reid go to the address shown when you confirm. Blockchain transfers can't be reversed, so check before you send.
  • Cashback appears in Wallet → Cashbacks and can be claimed at any time.
  • Report an unauthorised payment immediately at support@reidentity.net.
04

Virtual ID, cards and eSIM

  • Freeze a virtual card instantly from the card screen.
  • eSIMs are installed from Virtual ID → eSIM and work on eSIM-compatible phones.
05

Report a security issue

Found a vulnerability? Email security@reidentity.net. Please don't share details publicly until we've fixed it.
